THE MAN BEHIND THEM

Born in Denver, raised in Aurora. Rapping since I was twelve with Dre, Em, Bone Thugs, Tech N9ne on repeat in the headphones. Been carving my own lane ever since.

At 23 chasing radio took me to the Ohio Center for Broadcasting Colorado campus and I found out quick I'm a natural on both microphones. I ran a show, Living Mile High on GoMileHigh.com, but the music quickly demanded my full attention. I dropped The Truest Beginning in 2012 and I've been sharpening the blade ever since.
